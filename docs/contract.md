1. Strawberry milk pink pastel aesthetic — soft pinks, cream, light rose
2. Cartoon/playful illustration style with hand-drawn feel
3. Dark theme that maintains the strawberry milk vibe (deep berry, dark chocolate)
4. Responsive at both viewports (1440 desktop, 375 mobile)
5. Updated content reflecting PM role at 8seneca (1yr 3mo)
6. Fun, personality-forward — not a corporate template
7. Interactive prototype with working navigation between pages

- No build step
